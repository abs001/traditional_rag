MARUTI SUZUKI SWIFT: 1.2L Z-SERIES ENGINE OVERVIEW
========================================================================

1. ENGINE ARCHITECTURE & CONFIGURATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Engine Type: 1.2-Litre, 3-Cylinder Inline Petrol (Z12E)
* Power Output: ~80 bhp @ 5700 rpm
* Peak Torque: 111.7 Nm @ 4300 rpm
* Valvetrain: Double Overhead Camshaft (DOHC) with 4 Valves per Cylinder
* Fuel System: Multi-Point Fuel Injection (MPFI) with Dual Jet Technology

2. THE FOUR-STROKE CYCLE IN THE SWIFT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Intake Stroke: The piston travels downward, creating a vacuum. The 
  intake valves open, drawing a finely atomized mist of petrol and air 
  into the cylinder chamber.
* Compression Stroke: All valves close securely. The piston moves upward,
  squeezing the air-fuel mixture to a high pressure and optimal 
  temperature for combustion. Cooled EGR (Exhaust Gas Recirculation) 
  introduces managed exhaust gases to minimize engine knocking.
* Power Stroke: The spark plug generates a high-voltage electrical spark.
  This ignites the compressed mixture, causing a rapid, controlled 
  expansion of gases that forces the piston violently downward. This 
  linear force turns the crankshaft into rotational energy.
* Exhaust Stroke: The exhaust valves open. The piston moves back up, 
  scavenging and clearing the spent combustion gases out of the 
  cylinder toward the catalytic converter and exhaust system.

3. EFFICIENCY & PERFORMANCE TECHNOLOGIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Dual VVT (Variable Valve Timing): Continuously adjusts the timing of 
  both intake and exhaust valves dynamically based on engine load, 
  maximizing fuel economy at lower speeds and power output at higher revs.
* Idle Stop-Start System: Detects when the vehicle halts at traffic 
  lights in neutral. It cuts fuel injection to conserve energy and 
  instantly restarts via an integrated starter generator when the clutch 
  or brake is applied.
